Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

The short version

Daily Sprint is a brain-training game made by Atelier Sacha. It doesn't use ads, trackers or analytics, and it never sells or shares your data. The only things that ever leave your device are the nickname, country and scores you choose to put on the leaderboard.

Stored only on your device

Your game levels, best scores, score history, streak and sound setting are saved in your browser's storage on your own device. They are not sent anywhere. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes them.

The leaderboard

If you create a leaderboard profile, the following is stored on our leaderboard server (hosted by Supabase) and shown to other players:

  • Nickname you choose (please don't use your real full name)
  • Country you pick from a list (we never detect your location)
  • Scores with the date they were played, and your longest daily streak
  • A random player ID and secret key created by the app, so only your device can add scores or delete your data. They are not linked to your name, email or phone.

Deleting your data

Open the leaderboard, tap your name, then Delete my leaderboard data. This permanently removes your nickname, country and all your scores from the leaderboard server. You can also ask us to delete it using the contact below.

Children

Daily Sprint is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. The leaderboard only holds a nickname and country.

Fonts

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your device contacts Google's servers to download them. See Google's privacy policy for details.
